# Tic-Tac-Toe  
*Classic fun, right in your browser!*

---

## 👋 Welcome

Hey there!  
This is a simple, nostalgic Tic-Tac-Toe game I built using just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It’s perfect for two players-challenge a friend, a sibling, or even your nemesis (if you’re feeling dramatic). No installations, no accounts, just open and play!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **Two players** take turns clicking on the 3x3 grid.
- The first player uses **X**, the second uses **O**.
- Try to get three of your marks in a row-horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- If all squares fill up with no winner, it’s a draw.  
- Want to play again? Just refresh the page!

---

## ✨ Features

- **Instant gameplay:** Open the HTML file and you’re ready to go.
- **Clear, simple design:** Focus on the game, not distractions.
- **Automatic win/draw detection:** The game lets you know who won (or if it’s a tie).
- **Quick restart:** Play as many rounds as you want.

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

- **Start in the center** for the best odds.
- **Block your opponent** if they’re about to win.
- **Corners are powerful!** Try to control them.
